Wiccan Moon

Lunar Phases & Ritual Guide
☽ ○ ☾

Track the moon through the eyes of seven spiritual traditions. Wiccan Moon is the definitive lunar companion for the modern pagan — a considered, beautiful, spiritually literate tool that respects the traditions it draws from.

Features

  • Live moon phase with illumination percentage and zodiac position
  • Seven complete lunar naming traditions — Celtic, Wiccan, NeoPagan, Medieval English, Algonquin, Old Farmer's Almanac, and Pagan Deborean
  • Spell Timing Advisor — phase and zodiac-based ritual guidance with notable combination alerts
  • Lunar Gardening Calendar — planting guidance by moon phase and zodiac sign, monthly UK & Ireland calendar
  • Daily magical correspondences — herbs, crystals, colours, incense, tarot, and deity associations
  • Wheel of the Year — all eight Sabbats with full essays, clock-face visualisation
  • Supermoon and Micromoon detection
  • Moon phase calendar with daily detail
  • Ritual essays and seasonal guidance for every moon
  • Beautiful dark interface designed for contemplation

Privacy

No ads. No tracking. No subscriptions. No in-app purchases. No network calls. Your practice, your data, your moon. Location is used only for sunrise and sunset calculations and is never transmitted.
